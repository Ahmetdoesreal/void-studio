GPAF — Team Submission

Bind your deployed game back to your proposal, gather your players, attach logs, export one JSON. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Workflow: 1 import your GPPT proposal → 2 say how you realized each promised element → 3 set measurable targets → 4 attach your game logs → 5 validate & export. Players send their own files straight to the assessor.
