Step 2: Problem & Population

Problem Statement
In COM0463, some students know project terms but do not see how they connect. Scope, budget, team work, and quality can feel separate. This game makes them visible as office parts inside a void and targets about 15% better post-quiz results.
Target Population
Around 40 COM0463 IT Project Management students.
Success Criteria
  • At least 75% of students explain one good department choice.
  • At least 30 students play one full office run.
  • Each student finishes one minigame and buys one department.
  • Post-quiz results improve by about 15%.

Step 6: Narrative & Concept Mapping

Narrative Theme
The player starts with one desk floating in a dark void. Fast project minigames create main points, Budget, Team, and Quality. Departments like Scope Desk, Bug Lab, Sprint Floor, Risk Vault, and Stakeholder Booth appear when the player spends points. The final Portal Room opens when the office is strong enough. Self-Determination Theory is used because students choose upgrades. Mechanics-Dynamics-Aesthetics connects minigames with the feeling of building the office. Bloom Taxonomy moves students from terms to planning. Kirkpatrick four-level evaluation is used with reaction, quiz, transfer, and result checks.
Game Overview
The player builds an IT project office inside a void. Each round has a short arcade-style project minigame. Points buy departments. Budget, Team, and Quality change based on the player's department choices. When enough departments are built, the Portal Room opens and the office escapes.

Step 12: Preview & Export

In Scope (compassion model anchor)
The project includes a void office map, fast minigames, main points, Budget, Team, Quality, department buying, and losePoints=0 for failed cases.
Out of Scope
The project does not include real company data, VR, real money, online multiplayer, or advanced simulation.
Why (Motivation)
Students may care more when their project choices make the office grow and move them closer to escaping the void.
How (Ethics)
The game should use simple student IDs and avoid public ranking by real names.
